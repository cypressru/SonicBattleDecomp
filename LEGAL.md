# Clean-room policy

Contributions must be original source reconstructions based on lawful observation of the game
and public documentation. Do not commit or link to ROMs, extracted assembly, game assets,
proprietary executables, leaked source code, or tool output derived from confidential material.

Symbol names and types from another platform or build must be identified as such. Guesses must
remain visibly marked until independently corroborated. A byte match is verification of compiler
output, not proof of source language, linkage, naming, or provenance.
